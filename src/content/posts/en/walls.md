Walls is a real estate agency in Thailand. I built the front end of its website, which sells apartments in the Bellevue Lagoon residential complex in Phuket's Laguna area, five minutes from Bang Tao beach, at pre-sale prices during the early stage of construction.

## What I did

- **Pixel-perfect markup** with large interior renders that convey the atmosphere of the complex.
- **Responsive.** The landing page looks just as good on a phone, where it's mostly opened from ads and messengers.
- **Speed.** Optimized images so the heavy renders don't slow down loading.
- **SEO foundation**: titles and descriptions for Phuket real estate queries.
