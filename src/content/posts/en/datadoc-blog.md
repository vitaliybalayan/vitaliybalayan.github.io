The DataDoc blog is the service's media about documents and business: how to draft a lease or supply agreement, what to know about simple e-signatures, how to roll out electronic document management without stressing the team. Plus a separate feed of product releases.

For DataDoc, the blog is the main source of organic traffic, so it had to both look good and rank well in search.

## What I did

- **Design from scratch.** A homepage with a featured article and a feed of fresh posts, categories ("Good to know", "Documents", "Case studies", "Business", "Sales"), a curated selection, article cards with cover, tags, author, date, reading time and views. I paid special attention to the article page so long reads are easy on the eyes.
- **WordPress with a custom theme.** A bespoke theme built for the design, no off-the-shelf themes. Authors publish articles and releases through the admin, and the styling is applied automatically.
- **Custom scripts and routing.** Search, categories, tags, separation of articles and releases, clean URLs.
- **SEO.** Search-friendly structure and markup, metadata, internal linking between articles. The blog brings in people searching for templates and answers about documents.
- **Support.** Maintenance and improvements after launch.

## Result

DataDoc got a full-fledged content channel: the team publishes articles regularly, and they bring in new users from search.
