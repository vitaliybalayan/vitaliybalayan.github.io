BFL is an international bare-knuckle fighting league. Its tournaments draw thousands of spectators in the arena and hundreds of thousands watching on screens around the world.

I built the website entirely, from frontend to backend, while living in Thailand.

## What I did

- **Tournaments.** Event pages with the date, fight card and fighters.
- **Fighters.** Fighter cards with photos and details in a pop-up, in several languages.
- **Live streams.** The broadcast is embedded right into the site, together with a viewer chat.
- **Live giveaways.** A mechanic for viewers: numbers appear during the stream, and you have to add them up and be the first to send the answer while following the league on YouTube and Instagram.
- **Partnerships.** A section for sponsors with different partnership tiers.
- **Social and analytics.** Integration with YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, plus analytics.
- **Backend and admin panel.** Server side for fighters, events and content.

## Result

The league got its own website where viewers watch fights, take part in giveaways and follow fighters, and partners find the terms of cooperation.
