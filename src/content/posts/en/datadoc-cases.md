Case studies is the section of the DataDoc site with real customer stories: how a dental clinic, a kids' football school, beauty clinics, an education agency and a creative agency switched to automated document generation, and what it gave them.

Good stories sell better than any feature list, so the section had to look convincing and be easy to read.

## What I did

- **Design from scratch.** A case catalog filterable by industry (marketing, healthcare, education, sports, services), expressive cards with covers in each client's style, and a case page template where the story reads like an article.
- **WordPress with a custom theme.** A bespoke theme for the design. The team adds a new case through the admin, and it immediately appears in the catalog with the right styling.
- **Custom scripts and routing.** Industry filtering without page reloads, clean case URLs.
- **SEO.** Markup and metadata so cases are found for queries like "electronic document management for a clinic".
- **Support.** Maintenance and improvements after launch.

## Result

The third DataDoc section after the knowledge base and the blog. The sales team now has a showcase of real examples to show prospects from the same industry.
