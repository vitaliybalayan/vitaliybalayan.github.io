POLYUS manufactures X-ray protection: shielding structures for X-ray rooms, industrial radiation protection, cleanroom doors and protective clothing. The company has been on the market for 15 years and has produced more than 15,000 items.

## What I did

- **Custom WordPress.** No off-the-shelf themes: a bespoke theme for the design, a custom structure for the catalog, projects and articles. The team manages content in the admin on their own.
- **Product catalog.** Categories and product pages for healthcare and industry.
- **Trust-building sections**: manufacturing, completed projects, customer reviews, delivery, articles.
- **Leads.** Quick contact and quote requests, an online consultant on the site.
- **SEO.** Structure and markup for queries like "X-ray protection for an X-ray room".
- **Responsive.** The site is equally convenient on desktop and mobile.

## Result

The new site replaced the old one, which remains available via the "Old site" link. The company got a modern showcase and a convenient catalog, and I continue to maintain it.
