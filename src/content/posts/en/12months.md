12 Mesyatsev ("12 Months") is a chain of hardware stores in Kazakhstan. The app is built for regular customers: it replaces the plastic loyalty card and keeps shoppers up to date on promotions.

## What the app does

- **Loyalty program.** Every purchase at the checkout earns a percentage back in bonuses. The customer card is always on the phone, and its barcode is scanned straight from the screen.
- **Promos and deals.** Current store offers in a single feed.
- **Stores.** All locations of the chain on a map.
- **Push notifications** about bonuses and new promotions.

## My role

I've been developing the mobile app for iOS and Android since early 2023: building screens and flows, the integration with the loyalty server and notifications, shipping updates and fixing bugs.
