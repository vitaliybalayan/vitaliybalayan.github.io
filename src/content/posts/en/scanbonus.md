ScanBonus is an app for people who buy medicine and want to save on it. Scan a pharmacy receipt, get bonuses, withdraw them to your bank card. The client is QazAlliance, the Alliance of Pharmacy Chains of Kazakhstan.

## How it works

- **Upload a receipt.** Snap the receipt right in the app after signing up and linking a card.
- **Get bonuses in tenge.** The receipt is verified, and cashback is credited for eligible products.
- **Track the status.** The history shows which receipts were accepted, rejected or are still under review, and when the payout will arrive.
- **Pick the best deals.** A product catalog with filters by category, type and bonus amount: you can see in advance where the cashback is higher.
- **Withdraw to a card.** Accumulated bonuses go to a bank card in a couple of taps.

## My role

I've been running the app from scratch based on a ready-made design. The first commit, every screen and user flow, the server integration, publishing to the App Store and Google Play — all mine. I keep maintaining it: shipping updates, improving stability and performance, adding new features.

## Result

The app is live on iOS and Android and ranks in the top 40 of the Medical category on the Kazakhstan App Store.
