DataDoc is a cloud service that automatically generates template documents for companies and entrepreneurs: contracts, consent forms, certificates of completion. The knowledge base is where users find answers on how to use it on their own.

It's the first of three DataDoc sections I built end to end, from design to launch.

## What I did

- **Design from scratch.** Structure, navigation and visual style of the knowledge base. The homepage opens with search ("Type your question and we'll help you find the answer") and clear illustrated sections: getting started, templates, users and permissions, document storage, subscription and billing, batch generation.
- **WordPress with a custom theme.** No off-the-shelf themes: a bespoke theme for the design, custom logic for sections and articles. The team writes and edits content in the familiar admin without a developer.
- **Custom scripts and routing.** Article search, nested sections and tidy URLs. It works as a separate product inside the main site.
- **SEO.** Clean markup, metadata and a search-friendly structure, so answers about the service can be found right from search.
- **Support.** Maintenance and improvements after launch.

## Result

DataDoc users got clear documentation, and support got fewer repetitive questions. This section became the foundation for the blog and case studies I built next.
