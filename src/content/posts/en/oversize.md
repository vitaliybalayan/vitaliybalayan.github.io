OVERSIZE is a pizzeria in Saint Petersburg with huge pizzas, a bold personality and its own delivery. Its website is more than a menu showcase: it's a full online restaurant where guests choose a location, build an order and pay for it without picking up the phone.

I joined the project in June 2024 as a backend developer.

## What I did

- **Sync with the restaurants' management system.** Menu, prices, stop lists and orders are fully synchronized with the internal system the restaurants run on. If the kitchen runs out of an item, it disappears from the site. If an order is placed on the site, it goes straight to the kitchen with no manual re-entry.
- **SMS sign-up.** Sign-in and registration by phone number with an SMS code, no passwords or extra fields.
- **Online payments.** Card payment right on the site, with statuses that match for both the guest and the restaurant.
- **Support.** I maintain the project after launch: keeping it stable, fixing bugs and extending functionality.

## About the details

It's a commercial project, so I don't disclose the technical implementation details.

## Result

The site serves several locations of the chain: guests order and pay online, and restaurants receive orders directly in their system.
