Bolshaya Peremena ("The Big Break") is a Russia-wide competition for school and college students, run together with Rosmolodezh and supported by the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Science and Higher Education. It's more than a competition: tests that help you discover your strengths, a community of like-minded people and events all year round. Partners include leading companies, universities and federal children's centers such as Okean and Smena. Semifinals bring together hundreds of participants from across the country, and winners go, for example, on the "Dream Journey" by rail from Moscow to Vladivostok.

The website is the main entry point to the project: it explains the competition and its opportunities, publishes season news, showcases partners and leads to registration.

## What I did

- **Frontend.** The site is built on **Next.js** with on-demand revalidation. Pages are served pre-rendered, so they load fast and index well. When news is added or content changes in the admin panel, the affected pages update themselves without rebuilding the whole site.
- **Admin panel.** A convenient admin for the project team: news, sections about the competition and opportunities, partners. Content is edited without a developer.
- **Backend.** The server side that stores content, serves it to the site and connects the admin panel with the public site.

## Result

The site handles the attention of a nationwide audience, and the project team publishes news about every stage of the season themselves — it appears on the site almost instantly.
