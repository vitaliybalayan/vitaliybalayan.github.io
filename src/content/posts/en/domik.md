Domik.kz is a home services company in Kazakhstan: cleaning of apartments, houses and offices, deep cleaning after renovation or moving, window washing, upholstery cleaning and small home repairs (plumbing, electrics, furniture assembly).

I built both the mobile app and the backend.

## The app

- **Service catalog.** Popular services on the home screen, the full catalog and a detailed description of each.
- **Request in a couple of taps.** Pick a service, enter the address and the task, send. Request status and history live in the profile.
- **Phone number sign-in** with an SMS code.
- **News and promotions**: discounts, special offers, new services.
- **Push notifications** about request status and promotions.
- **Updates without the store.** Small fixes reach users immediately, without waiting for App Store review.

## Backend

The server side and admin panel for the team: services, requests, staff and their schedules, news and promotions, push campaigns, OTP authentication and app update management.

## Result

The service is live on the App Store: customers place requests in the app, and the team processes them in the admin panel.
