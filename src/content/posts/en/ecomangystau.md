Ecomangystau is the environmental portal of Kazakhstan's Mangystau region: news and initiatives, eco-education and youth programs, a map of pollution hotspots, air quality data, services and an online eco-consultant. The site is available in Kazakh and Russian.

I built the project end to end, and it's one of the longest-running in my portfolio: from late 2020 to this day.

## What I did

- **Design.** Portal structure, section navigation and visual style.
- **Frontend.** A homepage with news and initiatives, the "Hotspots" and "Clean Air" sections, services, the eco-consultant, responsive layout for phones.
- **Backend.** The server side: content, news by category, multilingual support, data for maps and sections.
- **Admin panel.** The team publishes news and runs the portal's sections on their own, without a developer.

## Result

For years the portal has been the main platform for the region's environmental initiatives, such as the "Taza Mangystau" challenge and competitions for schoolchildren and young people.
